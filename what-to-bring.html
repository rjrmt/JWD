Common questions

Do I need my own gun?

No — everything is supplied. Private lesson students may bring their own firearm if preferred.

What should I wear?

Closed-toe shoes required. Comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing. Skip loose jewelry or dangling accessories.

I've never held a firearm. Is that okay?

Yes. Quenton works one-on-one at your pace — no crowded classes, no pressure.

What if I need to reschedule?

Call or text (754) 667-0784 as soon as you can and we'll find a new time.
